title: Builder — Slide Sequence — Hide / Unhide Slides
type: Feature
priority: M
status: pending
area: builder
---

Add the ability to hide and unhide slides directly inside the preview builder, mirroring the toggle already available on the My Presentations card.

**Behavior:**
- Hidden slides disappear completely from the main slide sequence in the builder.
- A separate "Hidden Slides" section or menu in the builder lists all hidden slides, where the user can unhide them to restore them to the sequence.
- Hide/unhide state is kept in sync with the card-level toggle — both controls read and write the same underlying data.
