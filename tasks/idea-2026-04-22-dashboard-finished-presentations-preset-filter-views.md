priority: low
status: pending
area: dashboard-ui
---

Instead of (or in addition to) manual filters, offer preset view buttons that apply a pre-configured filter combination with one click — e.g. "Recent" (last 7 days), "Shared" (has been sent), "By Company" (grouped view). Could be implemented as tab-style toggles or a saved-view dropdown above the card grid. Explore what the most useful presets would be based on real usage patterns.
