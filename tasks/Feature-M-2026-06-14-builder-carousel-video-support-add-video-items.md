---
title: Builder — Carousel — Video Support — Add video items to carousels
type: Feature
priority: M
status: pending
area: slides
---

Allow carousel components to include video items alongside image items. A video item should support a URL (YouTube, Vimeo, or direct MP4) and render an embedded player within the carousel slide. The builder UI should let the user add, remove, and reorder video items the same way as image items.
