---
title: Builder — Your Presentation — Hidden Slides — Visual and output behavior
priority: normal
status: pending
area: builder
---

When a slide is marked hidden in the Your Presentation section, two things should happen: (1) the Slide Preview should visually reflect this by graying out the hidden slide's preview tile, and (2) hidden slides must be excluded from the assembled presentation output — instead, they should be accessible as extras via a burger menu on the last slide.
