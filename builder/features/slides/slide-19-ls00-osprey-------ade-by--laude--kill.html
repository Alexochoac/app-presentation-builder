2D Area Scan Distortion Measurement

Unlike conventional line-scan systems that measure in a single dimension, Osprey uses 2D area scan technology to analyse distortion in all directions simultaneously. Sub-pixel resolution detects roller waves, edge lift, and centre kinks across 100% of every lite.

  • Dense coverage oversampling — every area scanned multiple times
  • Vibration-insensitive: high-speed cameras freeze motion on the line
  • Intensity & geometry-based analysis for sub-pixel precision

Anisotropy & Iridescence Detection

Anisotropy causes iridescence when unbalanced residual temper stress selectively reflects polarised light. Osprey measures the rotation of polarised light in nanometres alongside all distortion types — the only system on the market to do both together.

  • Measures retardance / strain patterns in real time
  • Detects bowed or bi-stable glass pieces automatically
  • Side-by-side distortion + retardance map interface
Retardance map — high vs. low anisotropy comparison

Patented Optics & High-Speed Cameras

Osprey uses patented optics combined with high-speed cameras and powerful algorithms to deliver complete, precise, and accurate inspections — measuring optical distortion, anisotropy, belly bending, skunk stripes, surface quality, and geometry in a single pass.

  • Transmissive distortion measured in dioptres over 100% of product
  • Intuitive UI — data collected, stored and reportable per lite/batch
  • Works on flat glass, solar glass, laminates and plastic sheets
